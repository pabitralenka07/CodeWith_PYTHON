When writing an abstract for a seminar, it is important to follow a clear and structured format. The first step is to
ensure that the topic title appears at the top of the page, centered, and formatted in uppercase letters with a 14-
point font size. for clarity and emphasis. The abstract heading itself should follow the topic title, also centered,
and written in 12-point font size to maintain consistency. The body of the abstract should be written in a 10-point
font size and justified alignment, ensuring that the text flows evenly across the page without any irregular spacing.
The content of the abstract should be concise, typically between 200 to 300 words, and written as a single
paragraph. It should provide a brief summary of the research or discussion, outlining the purpose, methods, results,
and conclusions of the study without delving into excessive detail. This structured approach ensures that the
abstract is both professional and easy to read, giving readers a quick and clear understanding of the topic at hand.
